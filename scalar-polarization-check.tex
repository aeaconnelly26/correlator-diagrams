\section*{Scalar Polarization Check}

Default inward placement with one scalar-polarized leg and the others plain:

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Upper-left leg only} &
  \text{Lower-left leg only} \\
  \FourPointCorr[
    scale=0.72,
    show-momenta=false,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,plain,plain,plain},
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    scale=0.72,
    show-momenta=false,
    leg-styles={plain,scalarpolarized,plain,plain},
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ] \\
  \text{Upper-right leg only} &
  \text{Lower-right leg only} \\
  \FourPointCorr[
    scale=0.72,
    show-momenta=false,
    leg-styles={plain,plain,scalarpolarized,plain},
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    scale=0.72,
    show-momenta=false,
    leg-styles={plain,plain,plain,scalarpolarized},
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

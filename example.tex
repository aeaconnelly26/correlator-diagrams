\section*{Correlator Diagram Examples}

Default 2-point correlator:
\[
  \TwoPointCorr
\]

2-point correlator with \(q_i\) labels and Lorentz indices:
\[
  \TwoPointCorr[
    mode=q,
    field=\phi,
    indices={\mu,\nu},
    central=G^{(2)}
  ]
\]

4-point correlator with \(k_i\) labels:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    mode=k,
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=G^{(4)}
  ]
\]

4-point correlator with fermion lines and custom external labels:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    line=fer,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    leg-labels={\psi_i,\bar\psi_j,\psi_k,\bar\psi_l},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\]

4-point correlator with a visible center vertex, emphasized arrows, and scalar-polarized legs:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    blob=false,
    center-vertex-style=ringdot,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,scalarpolarized,plain,plain},
    momentum-arrow-size=6pt,
    momentum-arrow-sizes={11pt,8pt,5pt,5pt},
    propagator-arrow-size=8pt,
    propagator-arrow-sizes={12pt,9pt,8pt,8pt},
    field=\varphi,
    indices={1,2,3,4},
    central=\Gamma_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Vertex-style 4-point object with upper indices and no blob:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    mode=p,
    blob=false,
    field=\mathcal{O},
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta},
    index-placement=sup,
    central=\mathcal{V}^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Amputated 4-point correlator with endpoint vertices:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Amputated 4-point correlator with paired horizontal connectors:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=horizontal,
    field=x,
    indices={1,2,3,4},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1122}
  ]
\]

Amputated 4-point correlator with colored paired connectors:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=horizontal,
    leg-styles={{fer,fieldA},{plain,fieldB},{fer,fieldA},{plain,fieldB}},
    field=x,
    indices={1,2,3,4},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1122}
  ]
\]

Amputated 4-point correlator with a global line color:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=horizontal,
    line=fer,
    color=fieldA,
    field=x,
    indices={1,2,3,4},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1122}
  ]
\]

Amputated 4-point correlator with crossed paired connectors:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=crossed,
    line=fer,
    color=fieldB,
    field=x,
    indices={1,2,3,4},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1221}
  ]
\]

Amputated \(s\)-channel loop correlator:
\[
  \SChannelCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{s}
  ]
\]

\(s\)-channel loop correlator with left-in/right-out external momenta:
\[
  \SChannelCorr[
    external-momentum-flow=left-in-right-out,
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{s}
  ]
\]

\(t\)-channel loop correlator with left-in/right-out external momenta:
\[
  \TChannelCorr[
    external-momentum-flow=left-in-right-out,
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{t}
  ]
\]

\(u\)-channel loop correlator with left-in/right-out external momenta:
\[
  \UChannelCorr[
    external-momentum-flow=left-in-right-out,
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{u}
  ]
\]

\(\phi^4\) one-loop \(s\)-channel bubble with contracted internal indices:
\[
  \SChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{s},
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-top-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-bottom-momentum=p_1+p_2-\ell,
    loop-channel-contracted-indices={\alpha,\beta}
  ]
\]

\(\phi^4\) one-loop \(t\)-channel bubble with separate vertex and propagator indices:
\[
  \TChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{t},
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-left-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-right-momentum=q-\ell,
    loop-channel-left-label-xshift=-8pt,
    loop-channel-right-label-xshift=8pt,
    loop-channel-left-indices={m,\mu,n},
    loop-channel-right-indices={r,\nu,s},
    loop-channel-index-layout=tight
  ]
\]

\(\phi^4\) one-loop \(u\)-channel bubble with paired index lists:
\[
  \UChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{u},
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-left-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-right-momentum=r-\ell,
    loop-channel-left-label-xshift=-8pt,
    loop-channel-start-vertex-indices={i,m},
    loop-channel-internal-indices={\rho,\sigma},
    loop-channel-end-vertex-indices={j,n}
  ]
\]

Sunset self-energy diagram:
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    field=\phi,
    central=\Sigma(p)
  ]
\]

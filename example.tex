\section*{Correlator Diagram Examples}

Default 2-point correlator:
\[
  \TwoPointCorr
\]

2-point correlator with \(q_i\) labels and Lorentz indices:
\[
  \TwoPointCorr[
    mode=q,
    field=\phi,
    indices={\mu,\nu},
    central=G^{(2)}
  ]
\]

4-point correlator with \(k_i\) labels:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    mode=k,
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=G^{(4)}
  ]
\]

4-point correlator with fermion lines and custom external labels:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    line=fer,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    leg-labels={\psi_i,\bar\psi_j,\psi_k,\bar\psi_l},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\]

4-point correlator with a visible center vertex, emphasized arrows, and scalar-polarized legs:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    blob=false,
    center-vertex-style=ringdot,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,scalarpolarized,plain,plain},
    momentum-arrow-size=6pt,
    momentum-arrow-sizes={11pt,8pt,5pt,5pt},
    propagator-arrow-size=8pt,
    propagator-arrow-sizes={12pt,9pt,8pt,8pt},
    field=\varphi,
    indices={1,2,3,4},
    central=\Gamma_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Vertex-style 4-point object with upper indices and no blob:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    mode=p,
    blob=false,
    field=\mathcal{O},
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta},
    index-placement=sup,
    central=\mathcal{V}^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Sunset self-energy diagram:
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    field=\phi,
    central=\Sigma(p)
  ]
\]

\section*{Correlator Diagram Examples}

This file is the broad package tour. It starts with the shared controls, then
moves through the available topologies. The focused regression files cover the
edge cases in more detail.

\subsection*{1. Basic Entry Points}

Default 2-point correlator:
\[
  \TwoPointCorr
\]

2-point correlator with generated momentum labels, field labels, and a central
label:
\[
  \TwoPointCorr[
    mode=q,
    field=\phi,
    indices={\mu,\nu},
    central=G^{(2)}
  ]
\]

Default 3-point and 4-point correlators:
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{3-point vertex} &
  \text{4-point contact} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    field=A,
    central=\Gamma^{(3)}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    field=\phi,
    central=G^{(4)}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{2. External Labels And Line Styles}

Automatic \(k_i\) momentum labels:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    mode=k,
    field=\phi,
    central=G^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Manual external labels and a global fermion line style:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    line=fer,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    leg-labels={\psi_i,\bar\psi_j,\psi_k,\bar\psi_l},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Central blob hidden:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    mode=p,
    blob=false,
    field=\mathcal{O},
    central=\mathcal{V}^{(4)}
  ]
\]

\subsection*{3. Three-Point Vertices}

The 3-point slot order is upper-left, right, bottom. The default momentum flow
is upper-left incoming and the other two outgoing.
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Default flow} &
  \text{All incoming with styled leg} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=pho,
    field=A,
    central=\Gamma^{(3)},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=pho,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,pho,pho},
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-in,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    momentum-arrow-size=5pt,
    propagator-arrow-sizes={14pt,,},
    leg-labels={W_\mu,A_\nu,A_\rho},
    central=\Gamma_{WAA}^{(3)}
  ] \\
  \text{Amputated endpoint labels} &
  \text{Stretched geometry} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    external-vertices=true,
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma_{\mathrm{amp}}^{(3)}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    blob=false,
    center-vertex-style=ringdot,
    three-point-upper-left-xspan=1.75,
    three-point-upper-left-yspan=1.55,
    three-point-right-xspan=2.25,
    three-point-bottom-yspan=2.20,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-out,
    field=\chi,
    central=V^{(3)}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{4. Scalar-Polarized Legs}

Scalar-polarized legs are dashed scalar lines with an open marker. The marker
size follows the propagator-arrow sizing controls.
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Default scalar arrow} &
  \text{Larger scalar arrow} \\
  \FourPointCorr[
    blob=false,
    center-vertex-style=ringdot,
    show-momenta=false,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,plain,plain,plain},
    field=\varphi,
    central=\Gamma_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(4)}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    blob=false,
    center-vertex-style=ringdot,
    show-momenta=false,
    propagator-arrow-size=14pt,
    leg-styles={plain,plain,scalarpolarized,plain},
    field=\varphi,
    central=\Gamma_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(4)}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{5. Contact Pairings And Amputation}

Amputated external legs with endpoint vertices:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\]

Pairing controls for contact-style 4-point objects:
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Horizontal pairing} &
  \text{Crossed pairing} \\
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=horizontal,
    field=x,
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1122}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=crossed,
    line=fer,
    color=fieldB,
    field=x,
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1221}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

Per-leg color/style overrides versus a global line color:
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Per-leg styles} &
  \text{Global style} \\
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=horizontal,
    leg-styles={{fer,fieldA},{plain,fieldB},{fer,fieldA},{plain,fieldB}},
    field=x,
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1122}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    center-vertex=false,
    show-momenta=false,
    contact-pairing=horizontal,
    line=fer,
    color=fieldA,
    field=x,
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}_{1122}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{6. One-Loop \(s/t/u\) Channels}

The channel wrappers are fixed-topology versions of \verb|\FourPointCorr|.
\[
\setlength{\arraycolsep}{4pt}
\begin{array}{ccc}
  s\text{-channel} &
  t\text{-channel} &
  u\text{-channel} \\
  \SChannelCorr[
    scale=0.80,
    show-momenta=false,
    external-momentum-flow=left-in-right-out,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{s}
  ] &
  \TChannelCorr[
    scale=0.80,
    show-momenta=false,
    external-momentum-flow=left-in-right-out,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{t}
  ] &
  \UChannelCorr[
    scale=0.80,
    show-momenta=false,
    external-momentum-flow=left-in-right-out,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{u}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

Loop-channel momentum and contracted internal indices:
\[
  \SChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{s},
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-top-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-bottom-momentum=p_1+p_2-\ell,
    loop-channel-contracted-indices={\alpha,\beta}
  ]
\]

Separate vertex and propagator indices on the two internal lines:
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{\(t\)-channel} &
  \text{\(u\)-channel} \\
  \TChannelCorr[
    scale=0.88,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{t},
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-left-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-right-momentum=q-\ell,
    loop-channel-left-label-xshift=-8pt,
    loop-channel-right-label-xshift=8pt,
    loop-channel-left-indices={m,\mu,n},
    loop-channel-right-indices={r,\nu,s},
    loop-channel-index-layout=tight
  ] &
  \UChannelCorr[
    scale=0.88,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{u},
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-left-label-xshift=-8pt,
    loop-channel-start-vertex-indices={i,m},
    loop-channel-internal-indices={\rho,\sigma},
    loop-channel-end-vertex-indices={j,n}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{7. One-Loop Box Topology}

The box topology is a four-point loop with clockwise edge momenta. For this
topology the right-side external slots follow the box order: slot 3 is lower
right and slot 4 is upper right.
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
  \text{Default wavy box} &
  \text{No momentum labels} &
  \text{Mixed external styles} \\
  \BoxLoopCorr[
    scale=0.72,
    field=A,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{\mathrm{box}}
  ] &
  \BoxLoopCorr[
    scale=0.72,
    show-momenta=false,
    box-external-line=plain,
    box-line=plain,
    field=A,
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{\mathrm{box}}
  ] &
  \FourPointCorr[
    topology=box,
    scale=0.72,
    box-external-line=plain,
    box-line=plain,
    field=A,
    leg-labels={A_\mu,A_\nu,A_\rho,A_\sigma},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_{WAAA}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{8. Tree-Level Exchange Channels}

Tree-level exchange channels use the same external slots as the loop channels,
but their internal propagator uses the older \verb|channel-*| key family.
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
  s\text{-tree} &
  t\text{-tree} &
  u\text{-tree} \\
  \STreeChannelCorr[
    scale=0.80,
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    channel-momentum=\ell_s,
    central=\Gamma^{(0)}_{s}
  ] &
  \TTreeChannelCorr[
    scale=0.80,
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    channel-momentum=\ell_t,
    central=\Gamma^{(0)}_{t}
  ] &
  \UTreeChannelCorr[
    scale=0.80,
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    channel-momentum=\ell_u,
    central=\Gamma^{(0)}_{u}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\subsection*{9. Sunset Self-Energies}

Minimal sunset diagram:
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Sigma(p)
  ]
\]

Sunset diagram with explicit loop labels and contracted internal indices:
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    scale=1.35,
    field=\phi,
    central=\Sigma_{ab}(p),
    sunset-top-momentum=\ell,
    sunset-middle-momentum=p-\ell-r,
    sunset-bottom-momentum=r,
    sunset-internal-indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
  ]
\]

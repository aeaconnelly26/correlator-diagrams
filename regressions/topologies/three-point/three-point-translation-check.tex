\section*{Three-Point Translation Check}

Logical slots should keep their labels, indices, styles, and momenta while the
generic corner orientation key translates geometric placement.

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{\texttt{orientation=\{top,left\}}} &
  \text{\texttt{three-point-corner=\{left,top\}}} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    orientation={top,left},
    field=A,
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=default
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-corner={left,top},
    field=A,
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=default

  \text{\texttt{three-point-corner=\{left\}}} \\

Equation-style rows exercise the thesis-style wrapper in the compact format
used for algebraic rules.

\[
\begin{array}{rl}
  \ThreePointThesis[orientation={top,left}]
  &=
  \displaystyle
  \frac{ig}{m_Z}\epsilon^\alpha(q_1)(-k^*)\Gamma_{\alpha\lambda}(q_1)
  \\[3.5em]
  \ThreePointThesis[orientation={top,right}]
  &=
  \displaystyle
  \frac{ig}{m_Z}\epsilon^\alpha(q_1)(-k^*)\Gamma_{\alpha\lambda}(q_1)
  \\[3.5em]
  \ThreePointThesis[orientation={bottom,left}]
  &=
  \displaystyle
  \frac{ig}{m_Z}\epsilon^\alpha(q_1)(-k^*)\Gamma_{\alpha\lambda}(q_1)
  \\[3.5em]

  \displaystyle

\end{array}
\]

\newpage

The thesis-style wrapper still accepts normal three-point overrides, including
which logical slot carries the scalar-polarized leg and which momentum labels
are printed.

\[
\begin{array}{rl}
  \ThreePointThesis[
    orientation={top,left},
    leg-styles={{spol,magenta!85!red,endcap},{ewboson,endcap},{ewboson,endcapp}},
    momentum-labels={r,s,t}
  ]
  &=
  \displaystyle
  \mathcal{V}_{1}(r,s,t)
  \\[4.0em]
  \ThreePointThesis[
    orientation={bottom,right},
    leg-styles={{ewboson,endcapp},{spol,magenta!85!red,endcap},{ewboson,endcap}},
    momentum-labels={\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3}
  ]
  &=
  \displaystyle
  \mathcal{V}_{2}(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)
\end{array}
\]

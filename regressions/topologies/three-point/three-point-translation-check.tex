\section*{Three-Point Translation Check}

Logical slots should keep their labels, indices, styles, and momenta while the
orientation translates geometric placement.

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{right} &
  \text{left} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=right,
    field=A,
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=default
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=left,
    field=A,
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=default
  ] \\[3em]
  \text{up} &
  \text{down} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=up,
    field=A,
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=default
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=down,
    field=A,
    indices={\alpha,\beta,\gamma},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=default
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\newpage

The styled first slot and per-slot momentum directions should remain attached
to the same logical slot after rotation.

\[

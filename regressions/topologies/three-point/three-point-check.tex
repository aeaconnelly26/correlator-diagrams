\section*{Three-Point Correlator Check}

Baseline 3-point vertices. The first panel uses the default flow
\(q_1\) in, \(q_2,q_3\) out; the second uses the all-in preset with a
scalar-polarized incoming leg.

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Default flow} &
  \text{All in with styled leg} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    field=A,
    indices={\mu,\nu,\rho},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,ewboson,ewboson},
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-in,
    three-point-momentum-layout=outward,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    propagator-arrow-sizes={14pt,,},
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\newpage

Per-leg direction overrides and placement tuning:

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Upper-left out only} &
  \text{Longer annotation span} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3},
    three-point-upper-left-momentum-direction=out,
    three-point-right-momentum-direction=in,
    three-point-bottom-momentum-direction=in,
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,ewboson,ewboson},
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-in,
    three-point-momentum-start=0.12,
    three-point-momentum-end=0.84,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3},
    propagator-arrow-sizes={16pt,,}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\newpage

Crowded scalar-polarized vertices can opt into outward momentum placement:

\[
\begin{array}{c}
  \text{Incoming scalar-polarized leg with outward arrows} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    field={},
    leg-styles={scalarpolarized,ewboson,ewboson},
    leg-labels={W_\alpha,Z_\gamma,W_\beta},
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_3,q_2},
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-in,
    three-point-momentum-layout=outward,
    propagator-arrow-sizes={14pt,,}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\newpage

Geometry, labels, and amputation controls:

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Stretched ring vertex} &
  \text{Amputated endpoint vertices} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    blob=false,
    center-vertex-style=ringdot,
    three-point-upper-left-xspan=1.75,
    three-point-upper-left-yspan=1.55,
    three-point-right-xspan=2.25,
    three-point-bottom-yspan=2.20,
    momentum-labels={k_1,k_2,k_3},
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-out,
    field=\chi,
    indices={a,b,c},
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    external-legs=false,
    external-vertices=true,
    show-momenta=false,
    field=\phi,
    indices={1,2,3},
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\newpage

Cardinal orientation presets rotate the slot geometry while keeping slot
labels, indices, momenta, and styles attached to slots 1, 2, and 3:

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Orientation right (default)} &
  \text{Orientation left} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=right,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3},
    indices={\mu,\nu,\rho}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=left,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3},
    indices={\mu,\nu,\rho}
  ] \\[2.5em]
  \text{Orientation up} &
  \text{Orientation down} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=up,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3},
    indices={\mu,\nu,\rho}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    three-point-orientation=down,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={A_1,A_2,A_3},
    indices={\mu,\nu,\rho}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

\newpage

The \texttt{epspol} style combines a scalar-polarized external leg with the
double endcap. The special external slot can be shortened independently:

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \text{Short \texttt{epspol} external leg} &
  \text{Rotated \texttt{epspol} external leg} \\
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    leg-styles={epspol,ewboson,ewboson},
    three-point-external-length=1.05,
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-in,
    three-point-momentum-layout=outward,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={W_\mu,Z_\nu,W_\rho},
    propagator-arrow-sizes={14pt,,}
  ] &
  \ThreePointCorr[
    line=ewboson,
    leg-styles={{epspol,fieldA},ewboson,ewboson},
    three-point-orientation=up,
    three-point-external-length=1.05,
    three-point-momentum-flow=all-in,
    three-point-momentum-layout=outward,
    momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3},
    leg-labels={W_\mu,Z_\nu,W_\rho},
    propagator-arrow-sizes={14pt,,}
  ]
\end{array}
\]

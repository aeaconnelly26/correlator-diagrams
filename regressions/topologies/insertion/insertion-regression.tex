\section*{Semantic Insertion Regression}

Prototype bubble insertions on \verb|\TTreeChannelCorr| external slot 1.
The baseline keeps the existing tree-channel geometry unchanged.

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \insertcell{baseline t-tree}{
    \TTreeChannelCorr[
      field={},
      show-momenta=true,
      momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3,q_4,p}
    ]
  }&
  \insertcell{slot 1 bubble insertion}{
    \TTreeChannelCorr[
      field={},
      show-momenta=false,
      insertion=bubble,
      insertion-slot=1,
      leg-styles={{ewboson,endcap},{ewboson,endcap},{ewboson,endcap},{ewboson,endcap}},
      channel-line={ewboson},
      bubble-top-line={ewboson},
      bubble-bottom-line={ewboson}
    ]
  }
\end{array}
\]

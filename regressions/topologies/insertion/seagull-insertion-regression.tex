\section*{Seagull Insertion Regression}

Prototype seagull insertions on \verb|\TTreeChannelCorr| external slot 1.

\[
\begin{array}{cc}
  \insertcell{baseline t-tree}{
    \TTreeChannelCorr[
      field={},
      show-momenta=true,
      momentum-labels={q_1,q_2,q_3,q_4,p}
    ]
  }&
  \insertcell{default seagull}{
    \TTreeChannelCorr[
      field={},
      insertion=seagull,
      insertion-slot=1
    ]
  }
\end{array}
\]

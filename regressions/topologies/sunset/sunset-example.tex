\section*{Sunset Diagram Example}

Minimal sunset topology with labels and momenta hidden:
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    scale=1.35,
    field={},
    show-momenta=false,
    sunset-central-label={}
  ]
\]

Scalar sunset self-energy with external momentum \(p\):
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    scale=1.2,
    field=\phi,
    sunset-external-momentum=p,
    sunset-top-momentum=k,
    sunset-middle-momentum=p-k-q,
    sunset-bottom-momentum=q,
  ]
\]

The same macro also supports hiding momentum annotations while keeping external fields:
\[
  \SunsetDiagram[
    scale=1.2,
    field=\phi,
    show-momenta=false
  ]
\]

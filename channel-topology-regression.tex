\section*{Channel Topology Regression}

Contact topology:
\[
  \FourPointCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(4)}
  ]
\]

\bigskip

\(\phi^4\) one-loop \(s\)-channel with contracted internal indices:
\[
  \SChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_s,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-top-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-bottom-momentum=p_1+p_2-\ell,
    loop-channel-contracted-indices={\alpha,\beta}
  ]
\]

\bigskip

\(\phi^4\) one-loop \(t\)-channel with uncontracted internal indices:
\[
  \TChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_t,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-left-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-right-momentum=q-\ell,
    loop-channel-left-label-xshift=-8pt,
    loop-channel-right-label-xshift=8pt,
    loop-channel-left-indices={m,\mu,n},
    loop-channel-right-indices={r,\nu,s},
    loop-channel-index-layout=tight
  ]
\]

\bigskip

\(\phi^4\) one-loop \(u\)-channel with paired index lists:
\[
  \UChannelCorr[
    field=\phi,
    indices={a,b,c,d},
    central=\Gamma^{(1)}_u,
    momentum-labels={p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4},
    loop-channel-left-momentum=\ell,
    loop-channel-right-momentum=r-\ell,
    loop-channel-left-label-xshift=-8pt,
    loop-channel-start-vertex-indices={i,m},
    loop-channel-internal-indices={\rho,\sigma},
    loop-channel-end-vertex-indices={j,n}
  ]
\]
